# Portfolio typography

Manrope (variable, 400–800) and Instrument Serif (regular and italic), downloaded from Google Fonts on November 17, 2026. Latin WOFF2 subsets only. Their OFL license files are stored alongside the fonts. No font service or CDN is contacted at runtime.

`node scripts/vendor-portfolio-fonts.mjs` refreshes the assets. Review a refresh visually before committing: Google may update the underlying font files.
